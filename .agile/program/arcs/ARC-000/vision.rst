Vision
======

:ARC-ID: ARC-000
:Last Updated: 2026-02-24

One-liner
---------
A guardrailed, VS Code-friendly agentic agile framework that produces demoable value while keeping staging and execution clean.

Problem
-------
Teams forget process boundaries, lose context after a few sprints, and ship half-artifacts that cannot be verified.

Outcome
-------
Sprints become traceable, verifiable, demoable, and easy to resume across sessions.

Non-goals
---------
- Replacing human product judgment.
- Auto-execution without explicit human permission.
